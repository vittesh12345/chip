## 3. Questions for the reviewer

1. Is the SS 100C/1.60V corner a requirement? If yes, the reviewed layout fails by 6.131 ns with 516 endpoints and needs a period >= 13.33 ns or multi-corner optimisation (K-01). If no, which corner and period define sign-off? SPEC states none (K-07).
2. Is a 20 % I/O delay budget (1.44 ns at 7.2 ns, no load/drive/transition) appropriate? The TT critical path is in2reg and includes it; reg2reg alone closes at 5.85 ns (K-07).
3. Should the comparator be duplicated (research note R5)? A comparator stuck at "no mismatch" is latent; comparators and the OR-tree are not injected anywhere (K-02, K-03).
4. Should copy B get its own multiplier or a residue check (R4)? `neg_product` shows silent data corruption, and the shared product is on the fenced critical path (T-03).
5. Should `out_valid_q`, `fault_q` and `phase` be triplicated (R1, +6 flops by the note's estimate)? `out_valid_q` escapes in 200/200 trials; `fault_q` 1→0 is unmeasured (K-03).
6. Is the shared voter and `nxt` cone acceptable, given that the routed netlist also shares `clknet_leaf_22_clk` between `u_copy0/q[1]` and `u_copy1/q[1]` and `place1872` between the resets of `u_copy1` and `u_copy2` (K-02, T-05, D-93)? Should the audit fail when one clock or reset leaf drives two copies of a group?
7. Is the ORFS KLayout deck (FEOL off, no density, no fill, no latch-up) acceptable at this stage, or must full sky130 DRC (Magic or the full KLayout deck) and Netgen LVS be run before the layout is called clean (K-04)?
8. Is fault-stop with host re-send acceptable for the intended use, and where is the host protocol specified (T-02)?
9. Are the 20 um / 10 um separation targets adequate as geometry-only TARGETs without sky130 data, and should clock/reset distribution be split per copy (research note P6/P7; K-08)?
10. Should the "code 3 behaves as STOP" requirement be verified by a double-upset or unconstrained-copy task (m27; K-10)? Same for the m07, m20, m22 gaps.
