Assurance is the product.

Anyone can publish a detection. The question a defense buyer actually has is whether it survives being checked, so Vantage shows what separated the call from the benign case.

Benign formation

Real pair, real elements: GRACE-FO 1 and 2 hold their spacing because orbital mechanics puts them there. Neither one spends propellant to stay, and the engine reads it that way.

Shadowing

One party maneuvers to hold station next to another that never agreed to it. The burn ledger carries the asymmetry that separates a shadower from a neighbor.

Both charts are engine output, same discriminator. The benign case is real, GRACE-FO 1/2 from public CelesTrak element sets. The shadowing case is the seeded scenario, where the one-sidedness alone carries the call at 100%.
