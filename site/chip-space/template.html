ORBIT-AI · On-orbit AI compute

An AI chip for orbit. It catches its own mistakes.

ORBIT-AI is a Vantage chip design for running AI on board a spacecraft. This 346 µm demonstrator flags its own bit flips, then stops or repairs them.

Demonstrator · simulated evidence · not flight-qualified

Anyone can put a chip in orbit. Keeping it right when radiation flips its bits is the hard part.

Orbit adds three more problems. Nobody can visit to repair a part, heat leaves only by radiation, and every watt comes from solar panels. ORBIT-AI answers each one in the chip's layout.

01 · RadiationAnswer: duplicate + compare

A flipped bit stops the chip.

Results are kept twice. A mismatch halts it before a wrong result leaves.

02 · No repair visitsAnswer: triple voting

A flipped copy is outvoted.

Thermal state is kept three times and rewritten on the next clock.

03 · HeatAnswer: thermal guard

It throttles before it cooks.

Throttle at 80 °C, stop at 95 °C, recover at 70 °C.

04 · PowerAnswer: INT8 inference

Integer maths, more work per watt.

Small integer arithmetic does more work per watt than floating point.

Evidence

It catches its own mistakes. In the layout, not in software.

Four mechanisms, placed and checked on the routed chip. Then we flipped its bits on purpose to see what got through.

M/01 DETECTduplicated state

Duplicate and compare.

Every accumulator and result register exists twice. A comparator checks each pair every clock. On a mismatch the chip raises a fault and stops, so no wrong result leaves it.

  • 702 / 702injected flips ended in a fault-stop
M/02 REPAIRtriplicated state

Triple voting on thermal state.

The thermal state machine runs in three copies behind a majority voter. A flipped copy is outvoted and rewritten on the next clock.

  • 234 / 234injected flips repaired on the next clock
M/03 SEPARATEplacement fences

Keep the twins apart.

One particle track can upset neighbouring cells. So copy A and copy B sit in fences 242 µm apart, and every same-bit twin is at least 251 µm from its partner.

  • 0 of 262same-bit pairs touching, down from 13 before separation
M/04 COMPUTE4 lanes

Signed INT8 arithmetic.

Four signed INT8 multiply-accumulate lanes with INT32 accumulation. Timing closes at a 7.2 ns clock after routing, setup and hold met.

  • 1.1 GOPSINT8 peak: 4 MACs × 2 ops × 139 MHz

Product output · placement fences

Every protected bit is stored twice.

Copy A sits in the left fence, copy B in the right. The fences are 242 µm apart. Every same-bit twin is at least 251 µm from its partner.

Thermal state is kept three times.

Three copies in the centre column, at least 98 µm apart. A majority vote runs every clock.

Where it has to work

No repair visits. No second chance.

A part that fails in orbit stays failed. That is why the error catching lives in the silicon, where it cannot be skipped.

How it compares

Built to catch its own mistakes. Nobody else shows you how.

Flight processors are hardened, but they are not AI engines. The AI chips that fly do not publish how they handle upsets. ORBIT-AI does both, and shows its work.

Scope The 4-lane demonstrator is built: routed sky130 layout, DRC 0, LVS clean. Its fault handling is verified in gate-level simulation; radiation testing is the next step. The 16-tile full-size design, same architecture, reaches 6.55 to 26.21 INT8 TOPS on paper.
