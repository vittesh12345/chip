01 · Why orbit is hard

Orbit is hostile to chips. Four reasons.

01 Radiation One charged particle can flip a stored bit.

Answer: duplicate and compare. Results are kept twice. A mismatch stops the chip before a wrong result leaves it.

02 No repair visits Nobody can swap a part in orbit.

Answer: triple-stored thermal state. A flipped copy is outvoted and rewritten on the next clock.

03 Heat In vacuum, heat only leaves by radiation.

Answer: a thermal guard. Throttle at 80 °C, stop at 95 °C, recover at 70 °C.

04 Power Every watt comes from solar panels and batteries.

Answer: inference first, in INT8. Small integer maths does more work per watt than floating point.

03 · Radiation · cosmic rays, trapped protons

Particles hit. Bits flip.

Every protected bit is stored twice.

Copy A sits in the left fence, copy B in the right. The fences are 242 µm apart. Every same-bit twin is at least 251 µm from its partner.

Thermal state is kept three times.

Three copies in the centre column, at least 98 µm apart. A majority vote runs every clock.

05 · Four mechanisms

It catches its own mistakes. In the layout, not in software.

01 Detect

Duplicate and compare

Every accumulator and result register exists twice. A comparator checks each pair every clock. On a mismatch the chip raises a fault and stops, so no wrong result leaves it.

▸ 702 / 702 injected flips → fault-stop
02 Repair

Triple-redundant thermal state

The thermal state machine runs in three copies behind a majority voter. A flipped copy is outvoted and rewritten on the next clock. Throttle at 80 °C, stop at 95 °C, recover at 70 °C.

▸ 234 / 234 injected flips repaired next clock
03 Separate

Physical separation

One particle track can upset neighbouring cells. So copy A and copy B sit in fences 242 µm apart, and every same-bit twin is at least 251 µm from its partner.

▸ 0 of 262 same-bit pairs touching (13 before separation)
04 Compute

Signed INT8 × 4 lanes

Four signed INT8 multiply-accumulate lanes with INT32 accumulation. Timing closes at a 7.2 ns clock after routing, setup and hold met. Peak: 4 MACs × 2 ops × 139 MHz ≈ 1.1 GOPS.

▸ 139 MHz after routing · 1.1 GOPS INT8 peak
06 · How it compares

Far smaller than what flies today. Built to be checked.

Flight-proven processors are radiation-hardened but slow. Commercial AI chips are fast but carry no radiation rating. Newer space-grade parts such as PIC64-HPSC and XQR Versal close the gap. ORBIT-AI's pitch is the architecture: fault detection and repair built into the layout, and INT8 inference sized for an orbit power budget. The built demonstrator is tiny. The concept is where it aims.
