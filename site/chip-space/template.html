How it catches its own mistakes

Scroll · four mechanisms
DETECT

Duplicate and compare

Every accumulator and result register exists twice. A comparator checks the pair every clock. On a mismatch the chip raises fault and stops, so no wrong result leaves the chip.

702 / 702 injected flips → fault-stop
REPAIR

Triple-redundant thermal state

The thermal state machine runs in three copies with a majority voter, so a flipped copy is outvoted and rewritten every clock. Throttle at 80 °C, stop at 95 °C, recover at 70 °C.

234 / 234 injected flips repaired next clock
SEPARATE

Physical separation

A single particle track can upset neighbouring cells. So copy A and copy B are placed in fences 242 µm apart, and every same-bit twin sits at least 251 µm from its partner.

0 of 262 same-bit pairs touching (13 before separation)
COMPUTE

Signed INT8 × 4 lanes

Four signed INT8 multiply-accumulate lanes with INT32 accumulation. Timing closes at a 7.2 ns clock after routing, setup and hold both met.

139 MHz after routing
