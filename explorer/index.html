Die photo

The same routed geometry rendered the way a die shot looks: aluminium interconnect (sky130 uses Al for met1 to met5), TiN-like local interconnect, dark tungsten contacts and vias, polysilicon gates and the silicon under an environment light. Heights are true scale, so the die is as flat as the real one: the whole stack is 6.6 µm on a 346 µm die.

×1 (true) ×1 is the real stack height. Raise it to make the metal layers stand up; the chip in the corner says when heights are stretched.

Materials are approximations of the process (colour, roughness, reflectivity per layer); the geometry under them is the GDS. The colours come from thin-film interference in the oxide and passivation over each layer, which is why real die shots look gold, green, blue and purple; the tints are approximations, and the oxide glaze stands in for the dielectric the data do not model.
